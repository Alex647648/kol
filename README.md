## Quick Start

### 1. Prepare Input

```yaml
product:
  name: "Cloud South Pu'er Ancient Tree Tea Cake"
  price: "268"
  specs: "357g tea cake"
  selling_points:
    - "300-year-old ancient tea trees"
    - "Hand-rolled, sun-dried"
    - "Natural aging, improves with time"
  origin: "Jingmai Mountain, Yunnan"
  craft: "Hand-picked one-bud-two-leaves, stone-pressed"
target_audience:
  age: "30-50"
  profile: "Urban professionals with cultural taste"
stream_context:
  slot: "Love Life (daily commerce)"
  time: "Evening"
  duration: "12"
```

### 2. Run Through the Skill

Follow the SKILL.md pipeline to produce the **7-piece output set**:

```
1. Mode Assessment    → Knowledge-style, 3-layer value design
2. Persona Match      → Which persona facets to emphasize this session
3. Selection Score    → 5-dimension story score (target: >= 15/25)
4. Full Copy          → Golden 5-step structured sales copy
5. Stream Timeline    → Minute-by-minute broadcast plan
6. Interaction Presets→ 3-5 predicted comments + response scripts
7. Self-Check Report  → 4 checklists with pass/fail per item
```

### 3. Sample Output (Copywriting Excerpt)

> **Step 1 — Regret Resonance:**
> "How long has it been since you sat down in total silence?"
>
> **Step 2 — Knowledge Anchor:**
> "In 862 AD, the Tang dynasty book *Manshu* first recorded Yunnan tea..."
>
> **Step 3 — Sensory Scene:**
> "The second pour comes out amber. Hold it under your nose — dates, old wood, the smell of mountain soil after rain..."
>
> **Step 4 — Natural Transition:**
> "This cake comes from that exact mountain. Jingmai, 1400 meters elevation..."
>
> **Step 5 — Value Elevation:**
> "268 yuan buys you more than 357 grams of tea. It buys you a reason to slow down for twenty minutes every day."

## Methodology Highlights

### The Golden 5-Step Copywriting Structure

```
Regret Resonance → Personal Experience → Sensory Scene → Natural Transition → Value Elevation
```

Core principle: **Never mention product specs. Let scenes and emotions create desire.**

### 5 Rhetoric Weapons

| Weapon | Effect | Example Technique |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Parallel Structure | Rhythm + emotional escalation | "I haven't taken you to see..." x4 → "But I can..." |
| Space-Time Jump | Expand imagination | Daily life vs. distant world juxtaposition |
| Knowledge Anchor | Build trust + depth | Civilization history → everyday moment |
| Self-Deprecating Humor | Remove sales pressure | Self-mockery → absurd analogy → product punchline |
| Synesthesia | Awaken body memory | Sensory contrast (hot apartment vs. mountain moonlight) |

### Soft Conversion (No Hard Sell)

| Traditional | Knowledge-Style |
|-------------|----------------|
| "3-2-1 buy now!" | "If you'd like to try, the link is below." |
| "Only 5 left!" | "268 / 40 brews = less than 7 yuan each" |
| Countdown timer | Value reframing |
| FOMO pressure | Emotional identification |

### Story-Score Product Evaluation

5 dimensions, 1-5 points each, 25 total:

```
Origin Story + Craft Story + Cultural Story + Emotional Story + Sensory Story

>= 20: Perfect fit     → Priority scheduling
15-19: High fit        → Normal scheduling
10-14: Needs augment   → Anchor must create the story
 5-9:  Low fit         → Replace or use as filler only
  <5:  Not compatible  → Do not list
```

## Anti-Patterns (What NOT to Do)

- List product parameters and specs directly
- Use hard-sell tactics ("last X units", "countdown starts")
- Attack competitors
- Use unverified facts or misquoted classics
- Over-dramatize or force tears
- Play background music with countdown timers
- Rush products under 3 minutes each
- Accept products with story-score below 10
